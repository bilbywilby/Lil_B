# Governance

DevHound is currently maintained by its original author, who has final say over roadmap, releases, and brand decisions.

## Decision-making

- Small changes (bug fixes, doc updates, asset regeneration) can be merged directly by the maintainer after review.
- Larger changes (new brand elements, licensing changes, governance changes) should be discussed in an issue before a PR is opened.

## Becoming a maintainer

There's no formal process yet. Consistent, useful contributions are the best way to start a conversation about a larger role.

## Changing this document

Open a PR against `GOVERNANCE.md` with your proposed change and the reasoning behind it.
